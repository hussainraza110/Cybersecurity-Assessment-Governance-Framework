# Contributing

Thank you for considering a contribution to the Cybersecurity Assessment & Governance Framework.

The goal is to keep the project practical, evidence-led, traceable, and suitable for adaptation. Contributions should improve clarity or operational usefulness without overstating what a method or control can prove.

## Before proposing a change
- Check existing documents and [CHANGELOG.md](CHANGELOG.md) to avoid duplication.
- Explain the problem, intended audience, and expected benefit.
- Support technical or normative claims with authoritative sources.
- Distinguish requirements, recommendations, examples, assumptions, and local design choices.
- Do not submit confidential, proprietary, personal, classified, or otherwise restricted information.
- Do not include credentials, secrets, internal addresses, production hostnames, or unredacted evidence.

## Useful contribution types
- Methodology clarifications and documented assumptions
- Risk assessment criteria and scoring examples
- Policy, standard, procedure, and SOP improvements
- Assessment plans, evidence logs, risk registers, and findings templates
- Validation and remediation workflows
- Reference corrections, version updates, and broken-link fixes
- Sanitized examples demonstrating repeatable practice
- Accessibility, grammar, structure, and formatting improvements

## Documentation conventions
1. Use clear Markdown headings and descriptive filenames.
2. Define specialized terms and use precise language.
3. Distinguish **shall/must** requirements from **should** recommendations and **may** options.
4. Include scope, prerequisites, roles, inputs, steps, outputs, and limitations where applicable.
5. Record version/date information for material dependent on a standard or product version.
6. Put authorization and scope requirements before technical testing steps.
7. Separate observed evidence, analysis, risk, recommendation, and uncertainty in findings.
8. Label invented data clearly; ensure examples cannot be mistaken for real environments.
9. Link to external sources instead of copying substantial copyrighted text.
10. Update the changelog for meaningful changes.

## Review checklist
- [ ] The change has a clear purpose and fits the repository scope.
- [ ] Technical and normative claims are supported by reliable references.
- [ ] Assumptions, limitations, and version dependencies are documented.
- [ ] Commands and procedures are safe for the stated context and require authorization.
- [ ] Sensitive details are removed or replaced with synthetic examples.
- [ ] Third-party licensing and attribution have been considered.
- [ ] Links and Markdown formatting have been checked.
- [ ] The changelog has been updated where appropriate.

## Issues and pull requests
Open an issue or pull request at:
https://github.com/hussainraza110/Cybersecurity-Assessment-Governance-Framework

Describe the proposed change, why it matters, and the evidence or references supporting it. For procedural changes, include the environment and prerequisites needed to evaluate the proposal.

The maintainer may request revisions, defer a proposal, or decline changes that are out of scope, insufficiently supported, unsafe, or inconsistent with licensing and quality requirements.

## Contributor rights and attribution
By submitting a contribution, you confirm that you have the right to submit it and that it does not knowingly violate another party's rights or confidentiality obligations. You agree that accepted contributions will be distributed under this repository's [Attribution-Required License](LICENSE), unless a separate written agreement says otherwise.

You retain ownership of your contribution, subject to the license granted for its distribution in this project. Include appropriate third-party attribution and do not submit material whose terms conflict with the repository's license.

## Conduct
Be respectful, factual, and constructive. Critique ideas and evidence, not people. Harassment, discriminatory conduct, deliberate misinformation, and attempts to introduce unsafe or unauthorized activity are not acceptable.
