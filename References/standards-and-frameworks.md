# Standards and Frameworks Register

> **Instructions:** This reusable, organization-neutral template must be tailored before operational use. Replace bracketed fields, remove instructions that do not apply, retain relevant evidence references, and obtain approvals from the authority formally designated by the organization. Do not include credentials, secrets, or unnecessary personal or sensitive data.

## 1. Purpose and use

This register identifies external standards, frameworks, technical guidance and knowledge bases that may inform the repository. It is a navigation and traceability aid, not a declaration that any organization or system conforms to a source.

**Verification date for this register:** 2026-10-31. URLs point to official publishers or maintainers. A targeted official-source check confirmed the following details during preparation: NIST SP 800-61 Rev. 3 (April 2025); NIST SP 800-53 Rev. 5 Release 5.2.0 (planning note dated 2025-08-27); ISO/IEC 27005:2022 (Edition 4); CIS Controls v8.1; OWASP ASVS 5.0.0 (stable, 2025-05-30); and CVSS 4.0 (specification document version 1.2). Other references are linked to official publisher/maintainer pages, but their latest status should be rechecked before formal reliance.

## 3. Application guidance

- Select references according to the organization's sector, jurisdiction, contractual commitments, system type, risk profile and assessment objective.
- Distinguish normative requirements from informative guidance, catalogs, taxonomies, benchmarks and knowledge bases.
- Confirm the exact edition, amendments, corrigenda, errata and applicability before citing a clause or asserting alignment.
- Where a standard is paywalled, cite the official publisher metadata and use an authorized copy for detailed clause-level mapping. Do not infer clause wording from summaries.
- Record the date accessed and the exact version used in each assessment or policy mapping.
- Do not reproduce substantial copyrighted standards text in repository documents. Summarize requirements in original language and cite the source.

## 5. Important limitations

- ISO/IEC standards may require purchase; public landing pages do not provide the complete normative text.
- NIST publications may have updates, errata or superseding revisions; verify the publication page.
- OWASP projects, ATT&CK, CVSS, KEV, CIS Benchmarks and CSA CCM may change independently of this repository.
- The presence of a reference does not establish certification, compliance, control effectiveness or legal sufficiency.
