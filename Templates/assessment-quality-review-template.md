## 2. Planning and authorization checks

- [ ] Objectives, scope, exclusions and criteria are clear.
- [ ] Authorization and rules of engagement cover activities performed.
- [ ] Methods, sampling and coverage rationale are documented.
- [ ] Assessor competence and conflicts are addressed.
- [ ] Safety, escalation and evidence arrangements are defined.

## 3. Execution and evidence checks

- [ ] Execution remained within scope and authorization.
- [ ] Deviations, incidents and stop-work events are recorded.
- [ ] Evidence is relevant, reliable, sufficiently complete and traceable.
- [ ] Sensitive information is minimized and protected.
- [ ] Technical observations are validated and reproducible where feasible.
- [ ] Sampling conclusions do not overstate population coverage.

## 4. Finding and risk checks

- [ ] Each finding states condition and applicable criterion.
- [ ] Evidence supports the asserted condition and affected scope.
- [ ] Cause is not asserted without support.
- [ ] Technical severity and organizational risk are distinguished.
- [ ] Rating rationale and overrides are documented.
- [ ] Recommendations are actionable and proportionate.
- [ ] Risk owner and treatment authority are correctly identified.

## 5. Reporting checks

- [ ] Executive summary matches detailed results.
- [ ] Scope, methods, period, exclusions and limitations are disclosed.
- [ ] Finding counts reconcile to the register.
- [ ] References, evidence IDs and cross-links resolve.
- [ ] Management responses are accurately represented.
- [ ] No unsupported compliance or assurance claim is made.
