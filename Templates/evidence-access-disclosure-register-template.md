# Evidence Access and Disclosure Register

## 1. Purpose

Record material access to, export of, or disclosure of restricted assessment evidence. Apply the organization's access logging and privacy requirements; this register supplements, and does not replace, technical repository audit logs.

## 3. Review controls

- [ ] Requestor has a legitimate business need.
- [ ] Recipient identity and authorization are verified.
- [ ] Disclosure is limited to the minimum necessary evidence.
- [ ] Secrets and unrelated personal data are excluded or appropriately redacted.
- [ ] Approved encrypted transfer or access mechanism is used.
- [ ] Access expiry and revocation are defined where appropriate.
- [ ] Disclosure is recorded in required audit systems.
- [ ] Cross-border, contractual, privacy or legal restrictions are checked where applicable.

## 5. Exceptions

Record unauthorized access, accidental disclosure, misdirected transfer or suspected data exposure through the organization's incident response process. Reference the incident ID here; do not reproduce sensitive evidence in the incident narrative.
