## 4. Disposal verification

- [ ] Legal, investigation and contractual holds checked immediately before disposal.
- [ ] Disposal is authorized by the designated owner.
- [ ] All known copies and locations are addressed, including working copies and exports.
- [ ] Backup, immutable storage and third-party copies are handled under documented schedules.
- [ ] Approved sanitization/destruction method is used for the media type.
- [ ] Completion evidence or service-provider certificate is retained.
- [ ] Evidence Register status is updated without retaining prohibited content.
- [ ] Exceptions and residual copies are assigned an owner and expiry.
